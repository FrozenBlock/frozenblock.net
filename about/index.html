Who are we?

FrozenBlock is a minecraft mod development team created back in 2020 by LiukRast and now owned by Lunade. It became famous thanks to the Caves and Cliffs mod and from now on FrozenBlock is focused on creating big vanilla overhauls, with a lot of features, but keeping them in the same style/mechanics of Minecraft. We aim for our mods to fit within vanilla Minecraft.
