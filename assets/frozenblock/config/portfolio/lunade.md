hi

I own FrozenBlock! It took me two and a half years to get to that point, but it was worth it.

Since I officially joined FrozenBlock in 2022, I've been helping the team achieve a higher standard
for their mods and have been pushing them towards developing mods with a mindset that not only aims
to follow what Mojang puts out, but that also complements, respect, and blends in with Vanilla Minecraft well.

I am very feedback-oriented and am sure to always consult my team whenever a new, potentially risky 
idea comes up. Working with others to develop new and intuitive solutions within strict guidelines is something
I find very fun, and is something I believe truly fuels the creative process, especially for the mods I produce
at FrozenBlock which need to follow a strict philosophy and cannot break out of Vanilla Minecraft's design.

In my free time, you will usually find me modding Minecraft, writing/producing/recording music, or...
hopefully relaxing! I'm always free to chat as long as positivity is maintained, and am glad to accept new members
into our team if need-be.

The two main mods that show what I and my team are most capable of best are [Wilder Wild](https://modrinth.com/mod/wilder-wild) and [Trailier Tales](https://modrinth.com/mod/trailier-tales).
I am very proud to be the lead developer of both mods, and am always here to listen to feedback! I've spent lots
of time working on them, and I want to make them the best they reasonably can be for our players.
